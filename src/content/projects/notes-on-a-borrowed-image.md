A photograph is borrowed twice: once when it is taken, and again every time it is looked at.

This short film follows one image through several hands and several readings. It is an essay about circulation, permission and the small changes that happen when a picture is described out loud.

The film is intended to be shown alongside the photographs and documents that surround it, and is available as a captioned excerpt for online viewing.
