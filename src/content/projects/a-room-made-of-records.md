A room is never only a room. It holds the trace of everyone who has used it: the mark on a wall, the shape left by a chair, the paper pushed into a drawer.

This installation works with those traces as material. Furniture, printed matter and light are arranged so that the room reads as both a place and a record of one — a space that remembers.

The work was developed through a period of reading and conversation, and exists in different configurations depending on what the site can hold.
